# Application owns payment business time

The application layer defines the current time used for Payment lifecycle and idempotency decisions, and passes explicit timestamps through the `PaymentStore` port. The composition root wires one shared clock into the Payment Service and the idempotency replay cleanup worker. The application samples that clock at each business decision boundary and the Postgres adapter persists and compares the supplied timestamp, but does not own a clock or substitute its own current time; every PaymentStore entry point that accepts a business timestamp rejects a zero value. This keeps expiry, stuck-claim recovery, replay retention, and their tests governed by one explicit business-time policy rather than by whichever process happens to call the store.
